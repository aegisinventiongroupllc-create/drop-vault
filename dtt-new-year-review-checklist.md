## Protecting the idea

The live DTT entrance is already public. Protecting private files, IDs and account access is different from preventing someone copying the business idea. No security tool can prevent all copying or theft. Limit unreleased-detail sharing; use dedicated reviewer accounts, never administrator credentials; secure business, domain, email and vendor accounts with available two-factor authentication; rotate reviewer passwords after the review. A lawyer can advise on trademark protection, ownership agreements and confidentiality agreements. Do not assume an ordinary platform idea is patentable or an NDA binds a provider that has not signed one.
