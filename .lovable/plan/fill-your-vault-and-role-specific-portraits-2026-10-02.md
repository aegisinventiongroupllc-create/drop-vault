# Fill Your Vault and Role-Specific Portraits

## Customer dashboard
- Add a prominent **Fill Your Vault** section showing the customer’s current Bit-Token balance.
- Open the existing coin purchase screen when the section is selected.
- Add a clear **Go Back to Dashboard** action on the purchase screen.
- Read the balance from the customer’s account rather than temporary screen state, and refresh it after confirmed purchases so the dashboard always shows the credited total.

## Avatar privacy and creator likeness
- Keep customer selfie results distinctly cartoon-illustrated, discreet, and recognizable through broad traits without reproducing exact facial identity.
- Use the attached portrait only as the visual standard: clean dark outlines, friendly proportions, cel shading, expressive eyes, tidy hair and facial hair, and a polished social-avatar finish.
- Preserve full, wide, round, and other face shapes respectfully without slimming or cropping.
- Keep creator portraits more lifelike and identity-faithful so customers can recognize the creator.
- Determine customer versus creator securely from the signed-in account before generating the portrait; do not trust a browser-supplied role.
- Continue discarding original selfies and saving only generated portraits to the correct account.
