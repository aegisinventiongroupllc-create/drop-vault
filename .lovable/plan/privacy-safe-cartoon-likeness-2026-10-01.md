# Privacy-Safe Cartoon Likeness

## What will change
- Make every snapped customer avatar clearly cartoon-illustrated rather than realistic.
- Preserve recognizable broad traits such as face silhouette, complexion, hair, brows, eyes, nose, smile, facial hair, and glasses while removing identifying skin detail and tiny facial measurements.
- Handle very round, wide, full, or large faces respectfully: keep their recognizable shape, fit the full head and chin inside the circular portrait, and use balanced cartoon proportions without thinning, stretching, cropping, or exaggerating.
- Keep the same polished quality for women and men, with clean outlines, expressive eyes, layered cel shading, and a friendly profile-picture composition.
- Keep the selfie temporary and private; save only the generated cartoon portrait to that account.

## Technical details
- Tighten the server-side portrait instructions around privacy, non-photorealism, identity-safe likeness, and inclusive face-shape composition.
- Preserve the current Avatar Studio camera, loading, preview, fine-tuning, and save flow.
