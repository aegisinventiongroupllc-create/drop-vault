# Private Customer Emojis and Creator Profile Photos

## What will change
- Keep customer identity private with a discreet, polished 3D cartoon likeness based on their selfie.
- Preserve visible traits such as complexion, hair, eye color, facial hair, face shape, glasses, and visible facial tattoos without creating a photo-real copy.
- Match the expression in the submitted selfie, including cute, kiss, confident, playful, or intimidating expressions.
- Give creators a private photo gallery where they can upload, choose, replace, and delete their real public profile photo.
- Show the selected real creator photo in the discovery feed, creator page, Creator Dashboard header, and customer libraries.
- Continue using the creator’s generated emoji for private messages, heart reactions, and request conversations.

## Privacy and safety
- Customer selfies remain temporary and are deleted after the emoji is generated; only the generated emoji is saved to that customer’s account.
- Creator profile photos are account-owned and protected from unauthorized uploads, edits, or deletion.
- Creator photos are visible only to signed-in members through short-lived protected links.
- Raw emails and legal names remain absent from social views.

## Interface
- Keep the current simple customer flow: avatar name, Women/Men choice, Snap Your Face for Emoji, and Save Private Identity.
- Add a clearly labeled Public Profile Photos area to the creator dashboard with upload, active-photo selection, and delete actions.
- Maintain the existing dark neon visual style and mobile layout.

## Verification
- Test customer emoji generation and saved reload behavior.
- Test creator photo upload, selection, deletion, and protected display across all intended public creator areas.
- Confirm private interaction surfaces still use emojis rather than real creator photos.
- Run focused tests and verify the app build.
